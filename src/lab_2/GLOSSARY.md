# Glossary

Ubiquitous language for the Library Loans domain, taken from the librarian's description.

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Member** | A person registered with the library who may borrow books. |
| **Book** | A physical item in the library's collection that can be borrowed. |
| **Borrow** | The act of a member taking a book; it creates a new open loan. |
| **Loan** | The record that a specific member has borrowed a specific book. |
| **Due date** | The date a loan must be returned by: 14 days after the book is borrowed. |
| **Return** | The act of a member giving the book back; it closes the loan. |
| **Closed** | The state of a loan whose book has been returned. |
| **Overdue** | The state of an open loan whose due date has passed. |
| **Open loan** | A loan whose book has not been returned yet; a member may have at most 3. |

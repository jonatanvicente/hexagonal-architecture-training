## 📚 Recommended Reading

### Module 1. Fundamentals
- Alistair Cockburn, *"Hexagonal Architecture"* (2005), the original article — alistair.cockburn.us/hexagonal-architecture
- Alistair Cockburn & Juan Manuel Garrido de Paz, *Hexagonal Architecture Explained* (2024)
- Robert C. Martin, *"The Dependency Inversion Principle"* (1996, C++ Report)
- Robert C. Martin, *Clean Architecture* (2017)

### Module 2. Anatomy of the Hexagon
- Eric Evans, *Domain-Driven Design* (2003)
- Vaughn Vernon, *Domain-Driven Design Distilled* (2016), a short introduction
- Vaughn Vernon, *Implementing Domain-Driven Design* (2013), which includes a chapter on Hexagonal Architecture
- Herberto Graça, *"DDD, Hexagonal, Onion, Clean, CQRS… How I put it all together"* (2017) — herbertograca.com

### Module 3. Communication and Data Flow
- Tom Hombergs, *Get Your Hands Dirty on Clean Architecture* (2nd ed., 2023), a practical guide to ports and adapters
- Jeffrey Palermo, *"The Onion Architecture"* (2008), a blog series on a related style

### Module 4. Testing Strategies
- Ham Vocke, *"The Practical Test Pyramid"* (2018) — martinfowler.com
- Martin Fowler, *"Mocks Aren't Stubs"* (2007) — martinfowler.com
- Vladimir Khorikov, *Unit Testing Principles, Practices, and Patterns* (2020)
- Steve Freeman & Nat Pryce, *Growing Object-Oriented Software, Guided by Tests* (2009)

### Module 5. Real-World Use Cases and Advanced Patterns
- Martin Fowler, *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture* (2002), covering Repository and Unit of Work
- Chris Richardson, *Microservices Patterns* (2018), and the "Transactional Outbox" page on microservices.io
- Netflix Technology Blog, *"Ready for changes with Hexagonal Architecture"* (2020)

### Final Project. From Monolith to Hexagon
- Michael Feathers, *Working Effectively with Legacy Code* (2004), covering seams and characterization tests
- Martin Fowler, *"Strangler Fig Application"* (2004, updated 2019) — martinfowler.com
- Sam Newman, *Monolith to Microservices* (2019)
- Neal Ford, Rebecca Parsons, Patrick Kua & Pramod Sadalage, *Building Evolutionary Architectures* (2nd ed., 2023), covering fitness functions and how to prevent erosion
